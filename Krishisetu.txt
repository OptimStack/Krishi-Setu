PS Number:      SIH26132
Title:          Strengthening Market Linkages and Price Discovery for Farmers
Organization:   Government of Maharashtra
Theme:          Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development
Overall Verdict: GREEN (Strong Recommended Pick)

================================================================================
1. OVERVIEW IN PLAIN TERMS
================================================================================

Many farmers, especially smallholders and producer groups, have limited
visibility of current and expected prices across nearby markets, processors,
institutional buyers, and digital trading channels.

Why It Matters:
This affects farmer income, food security, and rural access to information
and markets. Even small efficiency gains in this system scale up to real,
tangible improvements in rural livelihoods.

================================================================================
2. THE REAL PAIN POINTS
================================================================================

  1. Limited Price Visibility
     Many farmers, especially smallholders and producer groups, have limited
     visibility of current and expected prices across nearby markets,
     processors, institutional buyers, and digital trading channels.

  2. Fragmented Market Information
     Information on quality specifications, demand, logistics, storage,
     payment reliability, and buyer credentials may be fragmented across
     multiple sources, making it hard for farmers to make informed decisions.

  3. Weak Bargaining Position
     Farmers may sell immediately after harvest because of liquidity or
     storage constraints, which leaves them with weak bargaining power
     against buyers.

================================================================================
3. WHAT THEY'RE ASKING FOR (FULL PROBLEM CONTEXT)
================================================================================

Many farmers, especially smallholders and producer groups, have limited
visibility of current and expected prices across nearby markets, processors,
institutional buyers, and digital trading channels. Information on quality
specifications, demand, logistics, storage, payment reliability, and buyer
credentials may be fragmented. Farmers may sell immediately after harvest
because of liquidity or storage constraints and may have weak bargaining
power. Buyers, meanwhile, may struggle to aggregate consistent volumes and
verify quality.

The challenge is to improve transparent price discovery and create reliable,
efficient linkages from farm gate to suitable buyers.

================================================================================
4. EXPECTED SOLUTION - KEY POINTS
================================================================================

Outcome:
  A market-intelligence and transaction-enablement solution that aggregates:
    - Mandi (market) prices
    - Buyer demand signals
    - Quality requirements
    - Arrival volumes
    - Transport and storage options

  ...and surfaces this information in a usable, actionable format for
  farmers and buyers alike.

Expected Outcomes Include:
    - Improved farmer price realisation
    - Reduced information asymmetry
    - Lower transaction costs
    - Stronger FPO (Farmer Producer Organization) aggregation
    - Reduced post-harvest loss
    - More reliable buyer sourcing

================================================================================

6. ARCHITECTURE - STRAIGHTFORWARD
================================================================================

Expect a typical 3-layer stack:

  Layer 1 - Data / Integration Layer
            (Mandi prices, buyer data, logistics data, storage data feeds)

  Layer 2 - Core Logic / AI Layer
            (Price prediction, matching logic, quality assessment)

  Layer 3 - Presentation Layer
            (Dashboard or mobile app interface for farmers/buyers)

Note: Most bugs and integration issues show up at the SEAMS between layers.
Budget real time for integration testing, not just for writing each layer
in isolation.
